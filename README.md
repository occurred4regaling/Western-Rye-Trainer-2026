## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Western Rye**. This trainer provides configurable advantages for Western Rye on Windows, including unlimited health, stamina, ammunition, supplies, money, equipment durability, and survival resources. It is designed for single-player use and supports exploration, crafting, farming, homestead construction, hunting, mining, fishing, and combat.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed single-player experience, faster access to crafting and building materials, easier survival management, or additional freedom while exploring the western frontier.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after entering the game world. Unlimited stamina supports long-distance travel, repeated actions, and horse riding without normal stamina limitations. Freeze time can be useful when crossing the desert, investigating abandoned settlements, or preparing a homestead.

### Tools & Inventory

Use item quantity and unlimited money options to obtain food, water, seeds, ammunition, ores, ingots, herbs, tools, and other supplies. Keep inventory-related options disabled while moving items between containers if the game behaves unexpectedly.

### World & Environment

Western Rye features habitable towns, NPC trading, abandoned locations, mining caves, wildlife, dynamic weather, farming, fishing, hunting, and homestead construction. Freeze time is suited to building, gathering, and exploration, while item and money options simplify access to equipment and trade goods.

### Combat & Weapons

The game includes revolvers, shotguns, rifles, infected Husk enemies, outlaw bandits, cultists, renegade factions, and bosses. Unlimited health, ammunition, no reload, and weapon durability are intended for single-player combat against these threats.
